# PRODUCT.md

MET-İŞ TALAŞ is an industrial wood-shavings manufacturer in Yurtbaşı, Elazığ. Public site: https://metistalas.com

## Audience

Primary: livestock operators on mobile phones — poultry breeders, horse farms / hippodromes, cattle and calf housing.

Secondary: purchasing managers and regional distributors.

## Brand personality

Industrial. Natural. Precise. Quietly established. Photographic. Contemporary white, not a marketplace flyer.

## Voice

Short sentences. Material language. No “supercharge”, no invented certifications, no capacity theatre.

Turkish is the default language of the site. English is a complete parallel, switched from the header.
